# Changelog

> English translation of [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md). The Chinese original is the authoritative version.

## 0.2.2 — 2026-10-09

The version after 0.2.1: each Operator's Potential and owned-Operator bonus are now set per Operator in "Operator Loadout" (default: max Potential, Elite 2 Lv.60); Japanese voices, Operator selection voices and a local Statistics page are new; a large batch of rules was corrected against the official data and PRTS (pushed and pulled enemies go into Unbalance, the teleporters on Leader fields, Dobermann's "Pointing Stick" pick-1-of-3, Raid jumping next to invisible enemies, etc.), the issues reported by the community and on GitHub were fixed, and PRs from several contributors were ported. The battle simulation now uses browser-independent math functions, so battle results are bit-for-bit identical across browsers and the server. Rules were checked against the official data and PRTS; see DESIGN §27 ([docs/history/0.2.2.md](docs/history/0.2.2.md)); intentional differences from the official game are listed under "Intentional differences from the official game".

### New features

- Potential and the owned-Operator bonus are now set per Operator, as in the official game: in "Operator Loadout" each Operator (shared by its Normal, Elite and Custom Squad versions) can be given Potential 1–6 and a Development level (Non-Elite / Elite 1 / Elite 2 / Elite 2 Lv.60), defaulting to Potential 6 and Elite 2 Lv.60 (the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-08: max Potential and full bonus by default). As in the official game, Potential changes deployment cost, redeploy time, stats and talents (summons' talents use the summoner's Potential); Development is the owned-Operator bonus from the "Bonus" section of the Dispatch Handbook, multiplying ATK, DEF and max HP separately rather than adding to other bonuses (Elite 1: ATK, DEF ×1.05; Elite 2: ATK ×1.1, DEF and HP ×1.05; Elite 2 Lv.60: all three ×1.1). Officially an unowned privileged Operator uses Potential 1 and no bonus; to play by the official rules, set it to Potential 1 and Non-Elite; stand-in Operators and Custom Squad prototypes have neither, and AI teammates use the defaults. Because the default includes the full bonus, the ATK, DEF and max HP of owned Operators are 10% higher than in 0.2.1 (0.2.1 had max Potential for everyone and no owned-Operator bonus). The settings sync with the loadout and are validated by the server; old loadout saves still work, with the defaults for anything unset; shop cards, detail cards, "In-Match Stats" and teammates' Operators all follow their owner's settings (GitHub #252).
- Field devices on the battlefield can now be tapped for a description too: Barricades, "Double-Eyelid" turrets, Ranged Tiles and Originium Flow Generators, with cards that look like the ones for special terrain; the numbers come from the device data of that stage (turrets also list HP, ATK, DEF and Attack Interval; blowers list the wind direction and the change to Operators' ATK and enemies' movement speed), and device names show the official translation for the current language; in battle the cards of Barricades and turrets show live HP, destroyed ones can't be tapped, and turrets not yet enabled and boxes removed by an Improv card aren't shown (GitHub #228, PR #229, thanks to @rickylxw).
- The top bar of the lobby and the room has a new "Settings" button left of "How to Play", so language, volume, hotkeys, etc. can be adjusted while waiting; it opens the same Settings window as the title screen and the match. Background music playback and board style options are not done yet (GitHub #238, thanks to @RushFTK).
- Selecting an Operator now plays the official "Operator selected" line: tapping an Operator in the battle area or on the Bench in the Rest Phase, or an Operator card in the shop or a Promotion Reward (before, only during battle); when you select several in a row the new line takes over the one being spoken, and during battle it doesn't interrupt more important voices such as deployment and skills. Deployment, skill, engage and settlement voices still only play during battle; stand-in Operators, prototype Operators, Alliance/Supportive Operators and summons have no official voices (requested by the maintainer on 2026-10-08).
- Japanese Operator voices: "Settings" has a new "Voice Language" with 中文 / 日本語 (Chinese by default, independent of the interface language). All 191 Operators with official voices have Japanese battle voices (the same 14 lines as Chinese: mission start, operation start, selected, deploy, in battle 1–4, settlement), and the few missing lines play in Chinese. The full bundle includes both the Chinese and Japanese voices (so it is about 76 MB larger than 0.2.1, about 505 MB); the lite bundle and the source download them with setup (Japanese about 85 MB, all assets about 550 MB) (requested by the maintainer on 2026-10-08; the multilingual voices proposed in GitHub #256 start with Japanese, thanks to @xcdoge).
- New "Statistics" page: "Stats" at the top right of the title screen and in the top bar of the lobby and the room opens the match records on this device — Overview (wins, win rate, Hidden Core reached / cleared, furthest round, total time, per difficulty, how many times each of the six titles was won), Strategies (matches played and cleared with each Strategy, clear rate), Combat totals, and Match history (one row per match; click a row to review that match's result screen; the latest 30 matches can be reviewed). The records live only in this browser and are never uploaded, and the server still keeps no history; at most the latest 1000 matches are kept (about 0.8 MB), "Export" and "Import" move them between devices, and teammates' callsigns from team matches are stored on this device too (both the page and gameplay guide §12 say so); a save that can't be read is set aside as a backup and recording starts over. Matches that reached Settlement, and matches with at least 1 round cleared before "Abandon Simulation", count in the stats; matches left right away before clearing the first round don't count, but stay in the match history marked "Left early" and "Not counted" (requested by the maintainer on 2026-10-08; GitHub PR #323, thanks to @2321Robin).
- The Operator list in "Operator Loadout" is now a table: one row per Operator, with the skill, the (Elite) Module and Potential · Development each in an aligned column; tap the skill or Module icon in the row to change it ("None" comes first), without opening the details first; tapping the avatar or name opens the details on the right, with Potential and Development at the top, followed by the Operator's Attribute (the same block as the in-match detail card, switching with Normal / Elite). Under 1000 pixels wide (e.g. a phone in landscape) the details cover the list; tap "Operator List" to go back; owned Custom Squad Operators set their Potential and Development on the slot card. The quick choices and Attribute preview come from PR #301, with the layout changed to a single table instead of three columns of stacked cards (the maintainer found the three columns a bit cluttered) (PR #301, thanks to @farridge).
- Opening the detail card of an Operator on the field draws its current attack range on the board: in the Rest Phase for Operators in your own battle area (by the skill, Module or stand-in it carries and its facing); in battle and when viewing a teammate's field, our Operators and summons (teammates' included) are drawn with their actual attack range from their current position, facing and skills (e.g. the enlarged range while a range-expanding skill is on); enemies, downed Operators and units on the Bench aren't drawn, and nothing is drawn while dragging or choosing a facing. Only the actual attack range is shown, with no skill range prediction (GitHub PR #281, thanks to @IceCodeNew; the idea of showing the range came from @BBleae).
- Co-op rooms have a new "AI Teammates Pick Last" option (off by default; when off the pick order stays random). (PR #368, thanks to @anAtheist987)
- Double-clicking a teammate's avatar in the Rest Phase opens their battlefield, and "Go View" is still there; it isn't forced open when there is no battlefield, and a single click no longer reopens it while you're already viewing. (PR #374, thanks to @shadiaojumao-hyb)
- Settings has a new "Report a problem" that copies diagnostics locally (optionally with this battle attached); player names are only replaced as whole names and as identical JSON strings. (PR #321, thanks to @Cloudnyco)

### Intentional differences from the official game

- The Champagne Bomb of Swire the Elegant Wit's S2 no longer loses HP (neither standing on Active Originium nor from any damage), can't be healed, and doesn't disappear when its HP reaches zero; it only disappears after its "welcome gift" blasts the first ground enemy that touches it. The official Champagne Bomb does lose HP (PRTS: even when its HP isn't full, no HP bar is shown under the model); this is the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-08.

### Rules (as in the official game)

- When three or more "highest same-name effect wins" effects (DEF reduction, Fragile, Weaken, Shelter, etc.) are stacked at once, once the strongest ends the next strongest one still running takes over in order, instead of skipping the one in between: e.g. 30%, 25% and 20% DEF reductions applied one after another (5 seconds each) — when 30% ends 25% takes over, then 20% (before, it jumped straight to 20%) (GitHub #342, PR #348, thanks to @siruimei07).
- The Module (REA-Y) of Executor the Ex Foedere and Entelechia, "ASPD +12 while 2 or more enemies are within attack range", is now handled by the battle engine: it takes effect the moment 2 enemies are in range and ends the moment fewer than 2 are (before, it could start up to about 0.2 seconds late and linger about 0.3 seconds); a scan of every piece, stand-in and Custom Squad combination confirmed no other Operator gains extra ASPD from this (PR #293, thanks to @LimitlessHPPK).
- The thunderstorm area of Passenger's S3 is now a 13-tile diamond centered on the target's tile (the official x-1 range; PRTS note), instead of a circle of radius 1.5 tiles around the target's position: enemies two tiles straight up, down, left or right of the target's tile are now struck too, and those beyond the second diagonal tile aren't (GitHub #322, PR #329, thanks to @IceCodeNew).
- The "net" at the end of Gladiia's S3 only drags enemies within 1 tile of the tornado's center (PRTS note: net radius 1), instead of reusing the tornado's 1.5 tiles; the tornado's slow and drag are still 1.5 tiles (GitHub #324, PR #329, thanks to @IceCodeNew).
- The reload of Sankta Miksaparato's S1 now goes to the most recently deployed Laterano Operator with an active ammo skill within the 8 surrounding tiles (PRTS note; redeploying counts as most recently deployed), instead of the one closest to him (GitHub #325, PR #329, thanks to @IceCodeNew).
- On the Leader battlefields of the Final Assault and the Hidden Core, the teleporters now teleport as in the official game: an enemy walking along its route into a teleporter entrance in a corner of the field no longer counts as a leak and costs LP on the spot; instead it vanishes into the teleporter for 3 seconds (the same as the teleport stated in the data), comes out of the teleporter exit in the middle of the field, and walks on to the blue gate on the entrance's side, counting as a leak only when it enters the blue gate; 4 official routes that omitted their "appear" step are fixed too, so enemies show up at the exit after passing through a teleporter instead of walking the rest of the way invisible. Teleported enemies pass the defense line a second time, so Leader fights play out differently (GitHub #336, PR #337, thanks to @2321Robin).
- The 【Doomsday Sermon】 of the Hidden Core's "OpFor: Gun": once the Gun-Breaker Spring has chased down to the Gun it no longer ends the chase immediately, but follows the Gun for the whole 5-second buff (invulnerable and not attacking meanwhile), matching PRTS; when there is no Gun left on the field it waits in place for the buff to end. The two Guns on a two-player battlefield still each cast by the data's cooldown (once 20 seconds after appearing, then every 45 seconds), and the one that calls later pulls every Gun-Breaker Spring to itself (PR #347, thanks to @CXUtk).
- The rush dispatch of the "Emperor" Strategy no longer has a 20-Stack cap: each deployment of an Operator halves its redeploy time again, and the 21st and later deployments keep halving (PRTS: this Strategy's effect stacks without limit) (GitHub #328, PR #329, thanks to @IceCodeNew).
- "OpFor: Bonespur" targets per its PRTS talent: its normal attack targeting ignores blocking (when blocked it picks targets by aggro priority instead of hitting the blocker first) and never attacks flying units, and the 3 targets it attacks at once while invisible are picked from its normal attack range (including the Operators' collision bodies) (GitHub #296, PR #365, thanks to @Sukvii).
- The capsule in the middle of the top bar counts the official way: it shows "resolved / enemies this round", where killing one and leaking one both count as resolved (starting at 0/3, leak one 1/3, kill one 2/3, kill the one that splits 3/3); enemies that split off and summons (including enemies summoned by a Leader) count in neither the numerator nor the denominator, so the capsule also fills up in Leader stages with summons, and is no longer "always a little short" as minions keep splitting; when they leak they still each cost LP and break a perfect battle, and kill stats and bounties are unchanged (4 Grindstones killed and the 8 that split off all leaking: capsule 4/4, 8 LP lost). The capsules for teammates' progress and in spectating count the same way (PR #308, i.e. the capsule part of PR #157, thanks to @xcdoge).
- When Stainless's "Prototype of Feist's Metal Crab" is within an Operator's attack range or skill range, skills that can act on it fire automatically as if it were an enemy and spend ammo: e.g. with only the Metal Crab in range, Executor the Ex Foedere's S2 activates and its bullets hit the Metal Crab, and the Laterano and Foresight Attributes "for every 7 bullets spent" stack as usual (before, the skill never activated, and neither bullets nor Stacks moved); Lemuen's S3 locks onto it and empties its ammo, Irene's S3 and Ch'en's S2 slash it, and the alchemy units of Thorns the Lodestar's S2 land on it. Skills whose effect only picks enemies and can't hit it (such as Texas's "Sword Rain") and healing skills don't activate for it, saving themselves for real enemies; the Stainless™ Multifunctional Platform still can't be attacked (community feedback; confirmed by the maintainer on 2026-10-08 per the official experience).
- Ines's S3 "Solitary Return" activates again after landing from a Raid redeploy (ATK up, recalling the shadow sentinel), instead of being swallowed by the jump; skills with an SP cost still keep their SP per "keep SP" (community feedback).
- The HP recovery the alchemy units of Thorns the Lodestar's S1 and S2 give to allies now raises the "HP regeneration rate", the same as Tin Man's "Big Larry" (PRTS Tin Man note: not affected by healing bonuses or heal prevention): Juggernauts (such as Vetochki) and Operators under heal prevention now regain HP too, and healing bonuses no longer amplify it (community feedback).
- Yan's Protection is a flying unit, so enemy effects marked "can't hit aerial" no longer hit it: the death explosions of the Infused Originium Slug, Infused Glacial Originium Slug and Cabbage Seedling, the Suiton Ninja's vortex, Boiling Pot's area damage, the prayer invitation of 'Sankta Head Held High' (ASPD −30), and the attacks of 'Armor Cleaver' and 'Armor Crusher' (community feedback).
- A Wang stone placed on the board now has its first talent "Mold Stones" deploy an extra stone (a follower stone) on an adjacent tile when deploying at battle start; it can land on undeployable ground, and its position follows the PRTS order (tiles with enemies first; undeployable tiles > deployable ground > deployable ranged tiles; ties broken by up > right > down > left of the stone); a follower stone disappears when a unit is later deployed onto its tile; the overflow follower stones when the S3 activates land by the same order. Before, there was not a single follower stone at battle start, and a lone stone never triggered (community feedback).
- When upgrading the Dispatch Center, the new shop slot appears immediately but stays empty, instead of rolling an Operator right away (official recording: upgrading only opens the slot, and Operators appear in the new slot at the next refresh — a manual Refresh or the automatic one at round start); the existing cards stay as they are; an empty shop slot is drawn as an empty frame instead of saying "Recruited". The "upgrading immediately fills a new card" behavior from 0.2.0 is withdrawn (GitHub #332, PR #333, thanks to @2321Robin).
- The "redeploy time −1.5% (Elite −3%) for every 3 【Kazimierz】 Stacks" that Nearl the Radiant Knight's Attribute gives herself and the Operator one tile in front of her is no longer capped at 5% of the base value: before, with very high Kazimierz Stacks (from 192 Stacks Normal, 96 Elite) the redeploy time stopped at 5%; now it keeps decreasing per the data down to 0 seconds (GitHub #370, thanks to @FrogThai).
- The Leader fight of Battlefield #08 (second half), Tide Controller: the patrolling "OpFor: Gun" and Lucien take the upper path when walking from the middle of the field toward the blue gate side (matching the official pathfinding), instead of crossing the two Deep Water tiles below. The pathfinding tweak that makes enemies "prefer paths where they can be blocked" now only applies when it doesn't step on more Deep Water, and existing routes on other battlefields are unchanged (GitHub #375, thanks to @FrogThai).
- Skills that take effect immediately and aren't an attack themselves (such as Thorns the Lodestar's "Wave Reckoning" and Vanguards' DP skills) fire at least one attack interval apart (firing doesn't use up a normal attack): the faster the attack speed the more often they fire, but however much SP Swift returns they never fire every frame (before, with high Swift stacks Wave Reckoning fired every frame and attack speed did nothing) (GitHub #298, thanks to @FrogThai).
- The Champagne Bomb of Swire the Elegant Wit's S2 can no longer be set off by invisible enemies that haven't been revealed: an invisible enemy only detonates it once revealed or blocked, and a sleeping enemy only once it wakes up (PRTS: invisibility makes a unit untargetable by any enemy ability; before, invisible and sleeping enemies set it off just by walking onto it, wasting the bomb on sleepers) (community feedback).
- The shells of the Imperial Artillery Targeteer and Imperial Artillery Core Targeteer: an Operator takes damage when its collision body (radius 0.25) touches the 1.2-radius impact circle, i.e. the impact tile and the ring around it, 3×3 tiles in all; before, only a cross of 5 tiles was hit (PRTS: collision check; GitHub #364, thanks to @FrogThai).
- A Marksman (trait "attacks aerial enemies first") standing on a melee tile and blocking ground enemies now hits flying enemies in its attack range first; other Operators still hit the enemies they block first (the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-08; GitHub #220, the idea of PR #220, thanks to @TsangAsuna).
- The 【Slow Simmer】 of the "Yu" Strategy no longer adds Stacks to the Alliances that don't show Stacks (Solo, Elite, Harmony, Assist Operator): in round 8, if only one of them is active it adds nothing, and if other Alliances are active too it only adds to those with Stacks (the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-08, differing from PRTS's statement that "these Alliances still take stacking effects"; the stacking from Assist and Warfarin is unchanged).
- Dobermann's Art "Pointing Stick" is back to the official pick-1-of-3: it is destroyed after use and pops up 3 tactical training bounties during the Rest Phase (only you can see them, and after a page refresh or reconnect they are still the same 3; drawn from 20 on Dire and Ultimate and from 15 on Standard and Perilous); tap one and tap again to confirm, and the chosen one becomes your next bounty; you can't click "Ready" before choosing, and if you still haven't chosen when the Team Rest Phase countdown ends one is picked at random (in Solo Simulation and with only one player the Rest Phase is untimed, so nothing is picked automatically); AI teammates and seats on AI Autopilot choose by their bounty score. Before (since 0.1.0) the server drew 3 at random and then took 1 at random, documented as an intentional simplification; 'Mystery Customer' is still a random bounty (PR #353, thanks to @Sukvii; the maintainer decided on 2026-10-08 to follow the official game).
- Tactical training bounty rewards are now paid in the Final Assault and the Hidden Core too: when your side has no leaks and the whole team wins, they're credited to the next round's Funds together with the kill rewards, once per card per player; before, these two battles only paid kill rewards — the tactical training enemies showed up and used up a battle, but the reward was never paid. The outcome of these two battles is reported by the browser and not re-simulated by the server, so the reward also requires the result to match what the browser reported during the battle: the report now splits the team's LP loss by player (the new leaksBy of b.progress; the rest is lost to the Leader's own effects), a player's list of leaks in the result can't be shorter than reported, and results that report LP loss without splitting it pay nothing, so leaks can neither be hidden nor pushed onto a teammate (PR #353, thanks to @Sukvii).
- Raid Alliance Operators also redeploy next to invisible (not revealed) ground enemies, and an invisible ground enemy in range counts as "an enemy in range" so they don't jump away; sleeping, untargetable and flying enemies still don't count (GitHub #316, thanks to @warpgenius; confirmed by the maintainer on 2026-10-08 by testing the official game).
- When a copy made by "Dusk's Scroll" completes a 3-for-1 promotion, the target's Equipment is now copied as usual: before, the promotion first returned the Equipment to the Bench, then the Scroll read an empty Equipment slot and the copied Equipment was lost (e.g. two normal Victorian Hammers should have merged into an advanced hammer with ATK +25%, but only the original +15% one was left). Now the Equipment is recorded before copying: the copied Equipment goes to the Bench just like the returned originals (PRTS: obtained Equipment is unequipped), same-name normal Equipment merges automatically as usual; the promoted Elite stays on the original tile with the same facing and no Equipment, and Equipment that is already advanced isn't put back on the Elite either (GitHub #389, PR #390, thanks to @Xinglan233).
- The DEF bonus of Thorns the Lodestar's S1 "Wave Reckoning" now stacks: when two alchemy units cover the same Operator it gets DEF +60 and +60 again (Elite +75), where before only one counted (the healing already stacked); the same goes for the few seconds when two alchemy units from two activations of the same Thorns the Lodestar overlap (PRTS note: all effects can stack; GitHub #388, thanks to @ChengCC0101).
- Pushed and pulled enemies go into the official "Unbalance" state, during which they don't walk or normal-attack. A push lasts from 0.2 to about 1.17 seconds depending on the "force minus weight" level (the displacement times of PRTS's approximate force-displacement table, e.g. 0.8 seconds for medium force pushing a weight-1 enemy), and a pull lasts 1 second (0.5 seconds when the force is 2 levels below the weight, and it lasts to the end once pulled up close); during Unbalance enemies also don't use skills, blink or do scripted moves, and Arc Frontliners take 400 true damage every 0.066 seconds during it (before, HP was taken all at once by the number of tiles pushed); aerial units too heavy to push (static rigid bodies) pause for 0.1 seconds when pushed and for the whole pull duration when pulled; levitation, rebirth and teleporting on the route all end Unbalance immediately. The push / pull slide on screen now uses the same time (half that in real time at the default 2× speed) (the idea of PR #392, thanks to @xcdoge).
- Equipment copied by "Dusk's Scroll" always goes to the Bench unequipped (to the Temporary Bench when the Bench is full, and destroyed with a notice like other obtained Equipment when both are full), and is no longer worn by the copied Operator — also when no promotion is triggered (PRTS Dusk's Scroll note: destroyed after use, obtained Equipment is unequipped).
- When several Operators have the same HP ratio, the alchemy unit of Thorns the Lodestar's S1 "Wave Reckoning" goes to the most recently deployed one (redeployed ones count by their new deployment), instead of the one with more Block closer to her (PRTS note: prefers the allied unit with the lowest HP ratio > most recently deployed).
- Attack intervals and SP costs that are exactly a whole number of frames no longer wait 1 extra frame: before, a 1-second attack interval actually fired every 31 frames (officially 30), 3 seconds took 91 frames and 4 seconds 121, and 10 SP from 0 at 1 SP per second took 301 frames to fill; enemies' attack wind-ups of exactly a whole number of frames are on time too (e.g. a 0.4-second wind-up is 12 frames, before 13), and so are the "Double-Eyelid" turret and the icicles of Santalla's S2. Both Operators and enemies are affected, and more enemies have whole-second attack intervals, so it's slightly harder overall. Intervals that aren't a whole number of frames still count from the frame that crosses 0 (rounding up, e.g. 20.34 frames counts as 21), while the official game rounds to nearest (20 frames); that isn't changed this time (PRTS battle mechanics "frame alignment": one frame is 1/30 second) (PR #402, thanks to @Cloudnyco).

### Interface

- After clicking "Ready" in the Rest Phase the shop collapses automatically and the board enlarges to the whole field, and cancelling Ready expands the shop again, so there's no need to click "Collapse" by hand (collapse and expand can still be toggled by hand at any time). Dragging by the Operator's body isn't done yet (GitHub #138, thanks to @blackyang40-cmd).
- Three readouts under the HP bar (display only, no effect on battle): while an ammo skill is active (such as Insider's S2, 14 rounds in all), the SP bar becomes a segmented yellow ammo bar — one segment per round, losing one per shot and coming back on reload — and there is no longer a constant gold aura under their feet while it's active; Vigil's Wolfpack has a row of diamonds under its HP bar, with the lit ones being the remaining wolf shadows (at most 3), disappearing in tactical-point form; when Hoshiguma the Breacher enters "Self-Attachment" her green HP bar stops at 1 and the excess damage grows as a red bar on the HP bar (PR #303, #378, thanks to @IceCodeNew; PR #383, thanks to @Convey123).
- The same-name Operator card that Orchid's "HR Consultant" freezes on every manual Refresh now shows frost: before, the server already froze the card and kept it into the next round, but the shop bar only showed the Freeze button above, so players couldn't tell which card it was; the rule is unchanged, and another manual Refresh redraws it along with the rest (GitHub #354, thanks to @DuolaCCCCat).
- When the renderer takes more than 12 seconds to initialize and the simplified view is used instead, it no longer attaches its canvas to the simplified view late and steals clicks: on timeout, failure or leaving the page the initialization is aborted and the late view destroyed, and the whole host element is no longer cleared (which before could also delete a newer view attached later) (PR #302, thanks to @siruimei07).
- For skills that fire the moment the unit deploys (such as Utage's), the sound of the first activation used to get lost (the skill event arrived before the unit's appear event); now it plays once the unit appears; when joining a battlefield midway only "who is on the field" is replayed, without playing all the earlier death, deploy and skill sounds at once (PR #292, thanks to @LimitlessHPPK).
- With many units on the battlefield, refreshes of the cached atlases are staggered by this frame's update order so each frame refreshes about the same number of units, no longer causing periodic frame-time spikes when a random phase makes some frames especially busy; no change to visuals or battle (GitHub #327 step one, PR #334, thanks to @Cloudnyco).
- The sword qi of the Custom Squad Operator Ch'en the Dawnstreak's S3 "Chi Xiao - Heaven's Plaint" is now visible: as the sword qi moves forward and turns on the battlefield it draws a trail of sword light and a crescent facing its direction of travel (before, the sword qi moved and dealt damage in the simulation as usual, but on screen there was only a flash at activation and on hits); the sword light is a procedurally drawn approximation, not the official particle effect (maintainer feedback; PR #382, thanks to @Convey123).
- During an enemy's attack recovery (after the attack animation finishes, before it starts walking again) it now stands still on screen too, instead of sliding forward between two screen updates; when the recovery is interrupted by a push, fear, stun, invisibility or death it moves as usual. Display only, no effect on battle (PR #381, thanks to @ZiyueW1116; replaces #307 and #314).
- Pushed and pulled enemies now decelerate as they slide to the position the simulation computed, instead of appearing at the end of the path at once (before, a push of about 1.7 tiles took only a frame or two, and longer pulls teleported); the slide duration is this game's own choice (officially it's impulse plus friction, with no published duration), and the body of an enemy knocked out after a push stays where it was pushed. Flying enemies that die stay in the air until their death animation finishes and only drop as they fade out; the 1.6-second cap on death animations is relaxed to 8 seconds, so the death animations of 25 enemies such as Sal Viento Bishop Quintus are no longer cut short. Display only, no effect on battle (PR #380, thanks to @xcdoge).
- The Champagne Bomb of Swire the Elegant Wit's S2 no longer shows an HP bar (PRTS: even when its HP isn't full, no HP bar is shown under the model); its HP, the damage it takes and whether it can be healed are unchanged (maintainer feedback).
- The eyeballs of Operators such as Pepe, Qiubai, Entelechia and Swire the Elegant Wit no longer show through their eyelids when their eyes close (blinking, lying down): when more than one Operator with an eye mask is on the field, or the graphics quality isn't "High", the mask is turned off to save performance, so now the eyeballs are hidden for the frames where the eyes are closed (GitHub #177; the idea of PR #384, thanks to @Convey123).
- When an enemy's attack can't wind up in time (e.g. an attack made up after the display fell behind), the whole attack animation now plays from the start as long as the attack interval fits it: the Recidivist's iron ball is raised before it's smashed down, instead of only the smashing second half; attacks faster than their animation still play from the hit frame (#246, thanks to @TsangAsuna).
- "OpFor: Bonespur" switches to its other form (the B-group model) immediately when blocked or revealed, and back to the three-headed serpent when it turns invisible again; switching while attacking, stunned or frozen also changes the model immediately (GitHub #296, thanks to @FrogThai for the report; PR #365, thanks to @Sukvii).
- In Unite (two teammates defending together) and two-player Leader fields, when an enemy comes in through one teammate's half and leaks on the other teammate's half, the progress of that battlefield in the teammate view after the battle matches the capsule during the battle (e.g. 1/1) and no longer jumps back to 0 (a follow-up to the top-bar capsule PR #308: at the end of the battle the whole battlefield's numbers are published, instead of adding up the two players' numbers each clipped to 0)
- When opening Alliance details while spectating a teammate, the member count and highlights now match the numbers outside: before, spectating in the Rest Phase also marked the teammate's Operators on the Bench and Temporary Bench as "on the field". ### New features

- **Update bundle** `Stronghold-Protocol-v0.2.1-update.zip`: contains only the files changed since 0.2.0, to upgrade a folder installed from the 0.2.0 full or lite bundle to 0.2.1 without downloading the whole bundle again. Stop the server, merge the contents of the `Stronghold-Protocol` folder in the zip into the install folder, overwriting files with the same name, then start as usual: on start it first checks every program file against `MANIFEST.json` and deletes old files the new version no longer uses (files you changed are not deleted), then runs normally; if the folder isn't a 0.2.0 bundle install (e.g. 0.1.x, or program files were modified), it tells you to download the full bundle and the server doesn't start. ### Intentional differences from the official game

- Skills whose attack range grows once activated (such as Wild Mane's S2, SilverAsh's S3, Mostima's S3, Mint's S1, Młynar's S2, Angelina's S3, 39 in all) fire once an enemy enters the enlarged range, instead of waiting for enemies to walk into the original attack range; Underflow's S2 follows this too.

- Automatic skills that only act on themselves or allies fire as soon as their SP is full, no longer waiting for enemies: Skadi the Corrupting Heart's S2, Eyjafjalla the Hvít Aska's S1, Vigil's S2, etc. (a follow-up to GitHub #124).
- Skills whose effect works over a summon's range also fire when an enemy enters the matching range of a summon on the field: Magallan's S1 (the Soaring Dragon's range, including aerial), Ling's S3 (the cross around each summon, ground only), Raidian's S2 (Cycle's range, ground only) and S3 (Center's range, including aerial), no longer only waiting for enemies to walk into the Operator's own attack range (the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-06).
- Wang: in this mode the stones placed on the board used not to take up her held stock, so after the skill stacked to the cap of 7 stones it stayed blocked, and could only be placed once per match. Now stones on the field take up stock, a stone leaving the field returns to stock and redeploying in place takes one again, and with full stock one stone leaving drops it to 6, so the skill can cycle for the whole match (the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-06, differing from this mode's "doesn't consume the held amount" description).
- When Ulpianus is knocked out after the 【Move】 of his S3, he goes back to lie down at his own deployment position and redeploys there, instead of staying where 【Move】 put him (community feedback; the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-07. The PRTS rule is to redeploy where the Operator fell; this is an exception for his S3 only).
- The Crying Thief swapped in by the "Duck Lord" Strategy takes 1 second to turn invisible again after being unblocked (officially 0 seconds): after it stuns an Operator and gets released, the Operators around it have one second to attack it; Crying Thieves appearing elsewhere (Bounty enemies) still use the official 0 seconds (community feedback, GitHub #214; the maintainer's decision of 2026-10-07).

### Rules (as in the official game)

- When all 10 Bench slots are full, you can no longer buy or claim Operators (not even the third copy that completes a merge); as soon as a slot frees up, Operators on the Temporary Bench move in automatically (GitHub #82, thanks to @Hucxious).
- The Unite phase now uses the official Unite-only map instead of this round's battlefield, so water, boxes and special terrain don't appear (GitHub #41, thanks to @Hucxious, @shanzhaikabi).
- An enemy's attack only takes effect on the damage frame of its attack animation: if it is stunned, frozen, put to sleep, levitated, disarmed or feared before the damage frame, that attack is interrupted, e.g. by the brief stun of Vulpisfoglia's S3 (GitHub #187, #170).
- Mystic Casters (Indigo, etc.) store their charge at the attack check, no longer waiting an extra attack interval (GitHub #181, thanks to @Paper-Yuan).
- HP regeneration no longer counts as healing: the continuous regeneration from Bards (Civilight Eterna, etc.), Perfumer, Blemishine's S2, Suzuran's S3 and Tin Man works on Operators under heal prevention and those who "can't be healed by allies" too, and doesn't take healing bonuses (GitHub #96, #137; the way to tell them apart was proposed in PR #135, thanks to @SpiritedAwayCN).
- Raid: active passive skills (Texas the Omertosa, Utage, Skadi's S2, etc.) count as "skill ready", so members jump next to ground enemies immediately (GitHub #49).
- Vigil's Wolfpack has the official "tactical-point form": after losing its last wolf shadow it doesn't disappear, and comes back at full HP with 1 wolf shadow after 25 seconds. S1 only fires while the Wolfpack is on the field, and acts by the Wolfpack's state (bringing it back immediately in that form, adding a wolf shadow, or fully healing it at full stacks); S2 can fire in tactical-point form too, saving the gift for the returning Wolfpack.
- The trait HP recovery of Soloblades (Utage, etc.) now triggers on every instance of damage dealt, and isn't affected by their own heal prevention.
- Multiple Shelters on the same Operator no longer multiply; only the highest one counts (Utage, Yu, Muelsyse, etc.).
- The healing chain of Chain Medics (Papyrus, etc.) jumps within the 3×3 around the previous target, and passes through allies at full HP too.
- Centurions, Crushers, Push Strokers and skills that say "attacks all blocked enemies at once" attack at most as many targets as their Block count, with blocked enemies first.
- The Champagne Bomb of Swire the Elegant Wit's S2 is thrown onto the tile of an enemy within x-6 range, and is placed even when there are no enemies nearby (GitHub #116).
- While Sharpedge / Sinkingsand hold broken blades they only have one layer of ATK bonus, and the bonus disappears once the last broken blade is used, no longer growing higher with every hit (GitHub #107).
- The Touch summoned by the "Touch" Strategy (Field Medic): the extra healing of S3 is calculated separately and takes bonuses, the healing is only increased below half HP, and the Promotion and Transcendence triggers are completed; the stand-in Touch uses the same implementation.
- Warfarin's Module healing bonus also applies when the target is at exactly 50% HP (per the client data; Blemishine, Gummy, Saria, etc. are still below 50%).

### Battle

- Deployment at battle start goes by column: the leftmost column first, top to bottom within a column. When an Elite merges onto the field, its tile is chosen in the same order.

- In Unite, Operators already down at the end of your own battle take part in the Ægir devour.
- Dollkeepers are at full HP after every switch. The second of the switch is heal-prevented, so neither others' healing nor their own gets in.
- Resilient also applies to retreating and stand-in switching (per the correction in the PRTS Alliance notes: knocked out, retreating, switching between substitute and self).
- Natural SP recovery continues during stun instead of stopping (Rockrock's S2).
- Both the move-out and the return of Ulpianus's S3 are free redeploys: deploy effects trigger again, he doesn't go into redeploy, and Resilient and the Ægir revive aren't used up by this move. His SP is cleared on return. The devour bonus is still kept (different from the official game, kept on purpose; GitHub #33).
- Swire the Elegant Wit's S1 heals nearby allies below 70% HP even without attacking, about once per attack interval (different from the official "heals on the next attack", an intentional adjustment).
- After the coins of Swire the Elegant Wit's S3 reach 10, she waits for a ground enemy to walk into the tile in front of her before firing; further away, or with nobody in front, the skill stays on and the coins stay.
- Podenco's spore cloud radius is changed to 0.9.
- Pramanix the Prerita's S2 only freezes the protection objectives (blue gates), no longer any standable tile.
- "Ground Operators" are counted by melee class, not by the tile under their feet. A Gladiia on a ranged tile gets Resilient, the Frightening Victorian Hammer and Humus's Recycling; ranged Operators standing on ground tiles don't.
