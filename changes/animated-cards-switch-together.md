### Room cards switch to the animated illustrations together, with the official card dissolve

- In the room and the Grand Alliance room, every card keeps its picture until all the cards' animated illustrations are ready. They then start playing behind the pictures, and once the animation runs smoothly, the pictures dissolve away one card after another in quick succession: a bright white glow rides the edge of the official card's dissolve slowly up the card, shedding twinkling glitter that falls out of the card, the whole card glows white, and the glow fades into the animated illustration.
- A card whose illustration fails keeps its picture and does not hold the others back.
- On a computer where the animations never run smoothly, the cards keep their pictures instead of showing stuttering animations.
- Before: each card switched on its own as soon as it was ready, with a short fade.
