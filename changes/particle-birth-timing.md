## 2026-11-23

### Particles start when the game starts them

- Official effects: a particle now starts at the exact moment the game's engine would start it within a frame, not at
  the frame's end. Effects that keep a fixed number of particles alive no longer blink out for a moment (or, in slowed
  effects, for several seconds) when one particle is replaced by the next.
- Animated illustrations: Nightingale's "Iakhu of Flows" ring no longer loses pieces for seconds at a time; the same
  dropouts are gone from 19 other animated illustrations (glows, sparks, butterflies, leaves and stars), among them
  Civilight Eterna's "Condolence", Pozëmka's "Snowy Plains in Words" and Ling's "Towering is Cliff of Nostalgia".
