## 26. 0.2.1 — after the 0.2.0 release

0.2.1 is built on `feedback6`, branched from the 0.2.0 release (tag v0.2.0) and marked `0.2.1-dev` until it shipped (2026-10-07). Its
workstreams branch off it as `fb6-*`, each merged after review with its golden scenarios named. Each rule was checked
against the official data and PRTS; anything no source backs is marked [ASSUMED]. Where each is handled: the 突袭
landing on a teammate's half of a shared field → §26.1; 联防 back on the round's battlefield → §26.2; the 自选 picks in
the bond lists → §26.3; a consumable on a full carrier → §26.4; the 外勤医疗 Touch → §26.5; 凋亡 and stored charges →
§26.6; 贾维's 拉普兰德 → §26.7; .gitignore → §26.8; enemy flyers over raised tiles → §26.9; enemy animation states →
§26.10; battle messages while the browser prepares a battle → §26.11; the 频次 器物 and leader summons → §26.12; the
bond strip's collapse arrow → §26.13; every operator at full potential → §26.14; Esc in the 自选 picker → §26.15.

Decisions of the owner: 2026-10-07 — a 突袭 member may land on a teammate's half when both halves of the field are
taken (the two-helper 联防 field, the Final Assault / Hidden Core pair field), replacing part of §25.18.1; 乌尔比安's

### 26.1 突袭 may land on a teammate's half when both halves are taken (the owner's decision of 2026-10-07) — `sim/battle/players.js onFieldBoard`, `content/bonds/addon/battle.js raidTile`

- **Seen**: 0.2.0 (§25.18.1) let a member land only on its own board, on every field. Where both halves belong to
  players — the two-helper 联防 field and a Final Assault / Hidden Core pair field — a member whose target stood on the
  teammate's half stayed home or took a farther tile of its own half (reproduced: the right player's member with an
  enemy on the left telin (1,3) of 战场#01 stayed home; on the two-helper 联防 map the left helper's member with an enemy
  at (10,15) stayed home). After the release the owner decided that such a member may land on the teammate's half.
- **Official**: the bond text 「保留技力立即再部署至一名地面敌人周围」 names no board, and where the official landing falls
  among the legal tiles is not in the data (§22.2). PRTS 卫戍协议/帮助 §联防阶段: with two helpers 「两处阵地将前后拼接为一处
  阵地，玩家在同一阵地作战」; a pair field is one boss map holding both players' boards (research 09 §1.2,
  ConvertChessPositionInfoToBossMap). A lone helper's other half and a solo boss field's other half hold no player.
- **Now**: `Battle.onFieldBoard(r, c)` — the tile lies on the board of a player of this field (`onOwnBoard` of any of
  `players`, the players whose units the battle holds; the 联防 right-hand helper's +8 columns and the mirrored right half
  of a pair are mapped as before) — and `raidTile` takes only those tiles: both halves on the two-helper 联防 field and on
  a pair field; the own half for a lone 联防 helper (escaped_single) and on a solo boss field (nobody stands on the other
  half; a teammate eliminated before the battle or playing another field opens nothing); never a boss field's hand row 0
  or 临时整备区 row 1 (no board covers them). Every other landing rule is kept: ground / ranged tiles (grid.canStand),
  free tiles (isReservedTile), a blockable tile first for a melee member (§25.11.3), a target only reachable from a
  forbidden tile skipped (the next candidate is tried, else the member stays); the candidates are still the player's own
  enemies first (raidTargets). 乌尔比安's S3 【移动】 keeps `onOwnBoard` (§25.17.2, a separate community report): his landing
  never takes the teammate's half. This replaces the own-half rule of §25.18.1 on those two fields.
- [ASSUMED]: none beyond §22.2's landing rule (which legal tile the official landing takes); with both halves open the
  nearest covering tile wins, on whichever half it lies.
- Golden: 8 scenarios, each parting from the parent at the first landing on a teammate's half (checked tick by tick with
  the parent instrumented: identical up to that landing): boss-boss_2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6-pair, hidden-boss_8 / 10-pair (the
  right player's 缄默德克萨斯, 伊内丝, 仇白 or 耀骑士临光 landing on the left player's half, 1–7 times per battle, the first
  0.3–39.8 s in) and the bot match coop4-ABYSS-7 (its round-6 two-helper 联防: at 52 s ai_2's 斯卡蒂 lands on ai_1's
  half; of the match digest only the two players' damage totals move, 8263 from ai_1 to ai_2). unite-2 is unchanged (its
  two landings stay on their own half). Tests: `test/sim/feedback5-raid-own-board.test.js` (4 cases; the pair and the
  two-helper cases fail on the parent; the solo boss field's hand rows, the lone helper and 乌尔比安 beside a teammate are
  unchanged). Docs: docs/SIM.md (`onFieldBoard`; `onOwnBoard` is now 乌尔比安's only).

### 26.4 A consume-on-equip item on a full carrier replaces first (GitHub #263, PR #264) — `match/player/items.js equip / _takeReplaced`, `ui/gameLogic/placement.js equipReplaces`, `match/bot.js itemTarget`

- **Seen**: with both equipment slots of an operator used, a normal 博士投影 dropped on it opened no replace dialog and
  silently destroyed the OLDEST equipped item: the client's `itemAttaches` treated every `consume_on_equip*` item as
  slot-free (`dropIntent` → `confirmReplace: false`), and the server ran the effect, whose `ev.keep` re-attached the item
  through `_attach(holder, item)` with no `replaceUid` — the full row pushed out index 0 (a `replaceUid` sent anyway was
  ignored). 盟约之币 and the other immediate consumables (随身身份牌, 紧急调度券, 精打细算玩偶, 简易通讯机, 见钱眼开玩偶, 骑士储蓄罐,
  寻呼模块, 信标, 拟态物质, 人事部文档, the golden 博士投影) opened no dialog either and kept both equipped items
  (reproduced on feedback6 783bd30: `scratchpad/w6-r21d-notes/repro.mjs`; reported by @SpiritedAwayCN with PR #264).
- **Official**: PRTS 卫戍协议/帮助 §装备 (fetched 2026-10-07): 「每名干员最多可佩戴2件装备」, 「达到上限强行佩戴会改为替换装备：
  指定一件已佩戴装备替换为将要佩戴的装备，并销毁被指定的装备（可取消以中断替换操作）」 — no exception for the items the data
  marks 装备时销毁; research 04's "never take a slot" is a [DATA] reading of `kind`, not a waiver of the dialog. The
  reporter's first-hand account of the official mode: the picked item is destroyed first, then the item resolves, and the
  operator is left with a free slot.
- **Now**: server — `PlayerState.equip`'s consume path takes the item `replaceUid` names (else the oldest, as for every
  third item) off a full carrier before `onEquip` runs (`_takeReplaced`), so the effect sees the carrier without it (its
  bonds: 随身身份牌 / 简易通讯机 / 寻呼模块 / 拟态物质); once the effect went through the item is destroyed (`onDestroy`
  reason `replace`) and the slot stays free — the normal 博士投影 takes it (`ev.keep`) and no longer pushes another item
  out. A refusal (博士投影 of either quality on an elite: `BAD_TARGET 'already elite'`) puts the item back at its index:
  nothing is destroyed, the 博士投影 stays in the hand. 信标 destroys its carrier as before: the remaining item returns to
  the hand, the picked one is gone. Client — `equipReplaces(ctx, uid, target)` (gameLogic) opens the replace dialog for
  every item on a full carrier, a consumable too, except where nothing would be replaced: an attaching item that
  completes an item merge (`equipMerges`; the server's consume path never merges) and a 博士投影 (its promoting effect
  keys) on an elite. `itemAttaches` is gone. Bots — a consumable whose effect is the same on anyone (funds, 紧急调度券,
  人事部文档 …) goes to a carrier with a free slot first (`itemTarget`, PR #264); the targeted ones (博士投影, 随身身份牌,
  简易通讯机, 寻呼模块, 拟态物质, 信标) keep their carrier and, when it is full, replace its oldest item (bots send no
  `replaceUid`).

### 26.5 The 外勤医疗 Touch casts on its 5-2, and the map characters' card is an operator card (GitHub #260, PR #278) — `content/tokens.js touchKit`, `ui/detailPanel.js MapCharDetail / SKILL_TEXT_NOTES / skillTags`

  and 特性 on the record) and `resolveTrigger` widens operators' skills only, so their records keep DEFAULT; TokenDetail
