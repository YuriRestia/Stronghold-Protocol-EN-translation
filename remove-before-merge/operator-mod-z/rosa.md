# Rosa

## Mod Z Description

Attributes increased: ATK +700

Marks enemies that she deals damage to; On skill end, recover 17 SP for each marked enemy that died during the skill.

## Commentary

Rosa is pretty much powercreeped by Typhon at this point. She has a lot of catch up to do.

S2 is her ongoing DPS skill, and S3 is her utility skill mostly used for the Bind infliction or when the stage has ASPD debuffs. S2 has a long downtime of 80 seconds at skill level 7, while S3 is a somewhat more okay 34 seconds. Still a lot of time that you can't really wait in SPA.

Hence I proposed solving her SP issues with a "refund on kill" type gimmick that doesn't require her to do the last hit. It fully refunds S3 with 2 kills, and S2 with 6 kills. S3 is most likely the better skill for mob clearing, though it won't scale with ASPD.

Additionally, the baseline ATK increase of Mod Z is huge. This should comfortably beat Mod X (ATK +90, 160% ATK scale against enemies with weight 3 or higher) considering her base ATK is around 1100.
