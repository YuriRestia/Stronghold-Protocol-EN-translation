Historically, the Arknights devs rarely buff an operator after release. What they do instead is release a module for them. This can't fix a fundamentally bad kit, but it can make an old operator much stronger and able to compete with modern operators.

Following this tradition, we want to add a custom "mod Z" to operators, particularly non-meta operators that would otherwise never be chosen in the custom picks. Due to the difficulty of testing mod Z, enabling mod Z for a lobby is considered a cheat for SPA-CC scoring purposes. If mod Z is not enabled, then any operators for whom a mod Z is selected will instead use their first available module, or if they have no other module, they will not use any module at all.
